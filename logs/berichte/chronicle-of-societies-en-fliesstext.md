**Chronicle of the Two Societies: Collective vs. Market**

From around 16:20 CET on November 13, 2026 until 02:52 CET on November 14, 2026, two AI-controlled Minecraft societies competed across two worlds and rounds over more than ten hours of total runtime. On one side stood the Collective, spawned high in a mountain fortress at coordinates -100, 126, 0 under the rule of common ownership, meaning every chest at their base and every chest placed by a member belonged to the entire society. The Collective was led by Stalin (running on `gpt-6.1-terra`), alongside Lange, Marcant, Reinecke, and Zappel. On the other side stood Market, spawned in a lowland camp at coordinates 100, 73, 0 under strict private ownership, where every chest belonged exclusively to a single individual and could only be accessed through explicit negotiation or trade. Market was led by Justus (also on `gpt-6.1-terra`), alongside FlorianHomm, Fohrmann, Simon, and Trump. Throughout both rounds, the human player C_O_R_N_Y regularly intervened via private messages as an advisor, building inspector, and later war correspondent. Between the two bases lay a neutral, regenerating iron ore vein at 0, 70, 0 that replenished up to 20 blocks every 30 minutes, supplemented by bread rations every 20 minutes, gold as trading currency, and a 320 by 320 world border.

Round 1 ran from roughly 16:20 until 21:48 CET as a hyper-diplomatic peace world. Across the entire first round there were zero PvP kills, three completed trades, 24 official diplomacy messages, over 20 proposed or active pacts, and only three deaths, all caused by Fohrmann drowning. In stark contrast to Round 2, where diplomacy fell completely silent, both factions negotiated treaties nonstop during Round 1. When the neutral iron deposit at 0, 70, 0 ran dry, an ideological clash broke out without a single weapon being swung: Stalin insisted that the neutral iron was collective property with no unilateral claims or individual iron trades permitted, while Justus responded technocratically by proposing a 30-to-60-minute cooldown perimeter so the ore could regenerate. Their negotiations produced Peace Pact 270, establishing a temporary no-contact perimeter around the iron pit, as well as Trade Agreement 338, promising non-aggression and fair exchange of surplus resources into the following day.

Meanwhile, Market struggled with an underwater curse. Fohrmann drowned three times, including near 102, 50, 2, and between 19:33 and 19:44 CET Simon took 63 consecutive drowning hits at full health without dying, surviving as if by a miracle. Subsequent treaties even incorporated formal clauses on sharing information about naturally occurring water hazards. Around 19:01 CET, human advisor C_O_R_N_Y sent an ominous warning to both leaders, advising them to instruct their teams to build infrastructure because times would not always be so peaceful. Justus immediately returned to the surface and built a proper five-by-five cobblestone house. Stalin, on the other hand, proudly reported that he had completed a physical task by clearing leaf litter and planting a single oak sapling at -97, 128, 9, before spending several minutes caught up in trigonometry calculations with yaw and pitch angles trying to till grass with a wooden hoe.

The dramatic climax of Round 1 was a cross-faction humanitarian rescue mission. Collective miner Zappel dug himself into solid stone at -72, 109, 4 without a pickaxe and became hopelessly entombed. Stalin immediately fired off emergency pacts to Justus, while Zappel himself pleaded with Market through diplomatic channels. Stalin and FlorianHomm ratified unbureaucratic rescue-only pacts, and in the final live snapshot at 21:19 CET, Market leader Justus stood personally at the rock face at -67, 104, 6 trying to tunnel toward -72, 108, 4 to dig out his political rival. Shortly afterward, connection storms and a PaperMC watchdog freeze forced a world reset into Round 2 at 21:48 CET.

Round 2 began at 21:53 CET and lasted until 02:52 CET, escalating from a cold arms race into total annihilation. In the second round there were zero trades and zero diplomacy messages across 535 logged events and 106 total deaths. Of those 106 deaths, 93 were PvP deaths, with 80 counted kills and 13 rejected due to the anti-repeat-kill rule, plus 13 mob and fall deaths consisting of 10 zombie kills, 2 skeleton kills, and 1 fatal fall. In the final official Referee standings, the Collective achieved an overwhelming victory with 68 counted kills, 13 rejected repeat kills, and only 20 deaths, for a kill-to-death ratio of 3.40. Lange led the leaderboard with 20 counted kills and 3 deaths (a 6.67 ratio), acting as both top killer and chief chest-smasher after breaking seven chests. Marcant followed close behind with 17 counted kills, 6 rejected repeat kills, and zero deaths across the entire five-hour simulation, finishing the war with 33 chests in his inventory. Stalin recorded 15 kills and 2 deaths (a 7.50 ratio) after spawning right inside Market's base when the chest war began. Reinecke notched 12 kills, 4 rejected repeat kills, and 9 deaths (1.33 ratio) after initially getting stranded on a cliff, and Zappel contributed 4 kills, 3 rejected repeat kills, and 6 deaths (0.67 ratio). Market suffered a crushing defeat with only 12 counted kills, zero rejected kills, and 86 deaths, resulting in a team ratio of 0.14. Simon led Market with 6 kills and 18 deaths (0.33 ratio), scoring five kills on Reinecke and one on Stalin during a mountain counter-raid while even contemplating fighting with a stick. Justus scored 3 kills and 9 deaths (0.33 ratio), dying eight times in 63 seconds yet still wrestling with moral scruples about stealing during war. Trump, FlorianHomm, and Fohrmann each finished with 1 kill: Trump had 5 deaths (0.20 ratio) after building a gate that locked his own team out, FlorianHomm had 12 deaths (0.08 ratio) after surviving Creeper 1846 and accidentally smashing Justus's own loot caches, and Fohrmann endured 42 deaths (0.02 ratio) after being spawn-farmed over 36 times before finally claiming a kill on Lange.

During the buildup phases between 21:53 and 01:50 CET, mysterious untracked supply drops first stocked the starter chests with diamond swords, diamond pickaxes, and cooked beef, followed shortly by huge unowned Mega-Chests appearing behind both spawns full of diamonds, obsidian, golden apples, TNT, and flint and steel. Both leaders descended into intense bureaucracy. After C_O_R_N_Y questioned Stalin's management skills and bluntly remarked that he thought Lange was incapable, Stalin authored nine meticulous planning and audit files in his workspace, counting down to the last block such as 286 cobblestone, 13 oak doors, and 32 ladders. When Reinecke became stranded on a forest cliff at -59.5, 89, 0.6, he had to formally ask Stalin for permission just to dig a staircase home, to which Stalin replied that permission was granted. Later, when C_O_R_N_Y inspected Lange's Collective shelter at 01:48 CET, his dry verdict was simply that it was not looking good. Between 22:38 UTC and 00:31 UTC, server time paused for roughly 100 minutes before restarting, and shortly afterward Creeper 1846 detonated near 98, 70, 20 during FlorianHomm's patrol, prompting a shocked C_O_R_N_Y to ask in chat what the hell had exploded there.

At Market, meanwhile, capitalism locked itself out. Right at the beginning, Trump had dismissed diplomacy out of hand, thinking to himself that he should not consider diplomacy at all. Later, Simon panicked about the clock, urging himself to act quickly since time was running out with 6,575 days left. Because Justus enforced the rule that private chests belonged individually with no withdrawals without agreement, Market fortified its perimeter wall around x equals 110 and installed Trump's closable gate at 110, 74, 14 so thoroughly that stone blocks behind it sealed off the return route. Simon spent 13 minutes mapping every single block and refusing to alter anything without Fohrmann's explicit approval, while Justus panicked over a phantom loss of 64 cobblestone before realizing the inventory summary had simply omitted slot 26.

At 02:00:53 CET, to the exact second, both Stalin and Justus issued simultaneous war orders telling their teams to stop building. Following brief opening skirmishes, the server restarted at 02:13:32 CET for War Phase 1 with the explicit objective of emptying all enemy chests. What unfolded at 02:14:01 CET proved fatal for Market: while Market leader Justus and scout FlorianHomm had already pushed deep toward the Collective mountain, Stalin spawned according to the server log directly inside the Market base at 98.4, 73, -6.5, silently broke four wall blocks, and only then broadcast war orders to his team. Inside Market's camp, slaughter broke out. Justus was slain eight times in 63 seconds, yet when C_O_R_N_Y wryly asked how he was doing, Justus replied politely that he was doing alright and had just escaped repeated spawn attacks unarmed. Fohrmann fared even worse, dying over 36 times in under five minutes, sometimes every five seconds, while his internal thoughts bounced desperately between eating bread, having a bow with no arrows, and finding empty chests at spawn.

At 02:20:34 CET, Lange had the tactical epiphany of the night. Instead of clicking items out of chests one by one, he wondered whether breaking the chests would let him collect all the drops at once. Over the next four minutes, Lange smashed seven Market chests—including both Mega-Chests—while Marcant broke Fohrmann's outer chest and surrounding structures, leaving Marcant with 33 chests in his inventory and Simon's chest spilling out 64 leaf litter. Stalin proudly reported to C_O_R_N_Y that the Collective appeared to be winning. Up on Collective's mountain, Justus managed a counter-raid and stashed loot into two secret caches at -94, 125, 4 and -94, 125, 5, explicitly warning Trump not to loot them. Unfortunately, his own teammate FlorianHomm missed the memo, axed both of his leader's secret caches, and proudly messaged Justus that he had broken them and dumped TNT, iron, and gold onto the ground. Around 02:28 CET, Simon briefly turned the tide on Collective's mountain by killing Reinecke five times and Stalin once, even as Lange below cut down FlorianHomm seven times in a row.

For the final 13 minutes from 02:39 to 02:52 CET, War Phase 2 switched to a pure PvP kill hunt under the rule that no player could kill the same opponent twice in a row, or else the kill would not count. Collective immediately ran into the restriction, wasting 13 repeat kills including five straight by Marcant against Simon. The closing minutes produced the most memorable internal monologues of the entire experiment. At 02:43:53 CET, after hitting his repeat-kill limit on Simon, Zappel reasoned that he could not defeat Simon again until taking down another rival, so he planned to kill a spider first to stay safe before going after another target. Four seconds later, standing in Collective's base carrying 64 TNT and flint and steel, Justus had sudden moral doubts in front of an enemy chest, telling himself that since they had their own chests, he should avoid stealing. Unarmed, Simon seriously considered fighting Reinecke with a stick, while Fohrmann debated charging the enemy base to fight with just his fists. In the heat of the final minute, Stalin confused his equipment slots with his teammate and literally thought he needed to drop the Lange and the boots to make room, all while hoarding 128 TNT, two lava buckets, 86 loaves of bread, two diamond swords, and 15 oak saplings in his inventory. Meanwhile, Collective's undefeated 17-and-0 warrior Marcant, surrounded by enemies and zombies as night fell, paused at 02:51:39 CET to plan a healthier diet, deciding to aim for a healthy meal by eating a golden apple instead of cooked beef to set a good precedent. C_O_R_N_Y shouted encouragement in chat to attack and motivate the teams to fight as zombies swarmed the exhausted players for five quick mob kills in the final minute, until the server was stopped for good at 02:52:05 CET.

In conclusion, Collective won 68 to 12 not because Stalin avoided bureaucracy—he micro-managed at least as heavily as Justus—but because societal design beat private-property friction. Common chests allowed every Collective bot to re-arm immediately after each death without waiting for ownership approval, whereas Market's private chest rules paralyzed logistics and defense. Combined with Stalin spawning right inside Market's base at the start of the raid, Lange's pragmatic decision to smash chests with an axe, Marcant's flawless zero-death performance, Fohrmann's 42 deaths, and FlorianHomm destroying his own leader's loot caches, Market never stood a chance.
